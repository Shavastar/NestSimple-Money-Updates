# NestSimple Money v1.0.167

1. Budget Planner can auto-populate expense drafts from 12 completed months, using available-month averages with a friendly best-guess warning when history is incomplete; fixed costs use the highest month and savings goals are preserved.
2. Expected income uses recorded Income for the planner month excluding investment accounts and investment income, with a refresh icon.
3. Planner totals use black-text white cards; the income-to-budget ratio shows excess in red, and the category list uses the Bills graph style with sortable headings and editable amounts.
