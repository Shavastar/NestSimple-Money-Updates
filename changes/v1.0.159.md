# v1.0.159

1. Restored Add Bill by including the corrected bill-entry module compatible with the shared form factory.
2. After saving new records, Income, Transactions, bill and transfer lists refresh by date, most recent first, while retaining filters.
3. Charts display Date From and Date To beneath titles, using monthly, twelve-month or account coverage as appropriate.
4. Expense and bill payment forms provide guided category-and-amount split rows with totals and validation; splits feed existing budgets and reports.
5. Added optional expense Due Date, retained bill Due Date, and added editable payee/supplier dropdowns plus a bill-payment payee selector.
6. Recurring bills offer Number of Payments or Continuously, retaining existing recurrence and remaining-payment behaviour.
