# NestSimple Money v1.2.03

Fix updater stalls caused by Windows reusing a stale app process ID for another program. App session and lock identity now includes process birth time; legacy records older than the current process are discarded without stopping that process. Includes v1.2.02 chart totals and independent expansion.

The file-only installer restores the shared navy left logo/tagline panel and white right information area, with Show Files beside native Close on completion. It does not close or reopen the app.

Checks passed: real Windows reused-PID/session/lock regression, preservation of unrelated running process, package download/review and failure/retry gates, trend hover. Full interactive restart verification was stopped at the user's request; the restored installer appearance has not been visually verified.
